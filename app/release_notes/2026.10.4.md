# 2026.10.4 — 2026-10-25

A small follow-up to 2026.10.3: auto-discovery collects your newer cloud-login devices again, and the setting that was supposed to control that now actually works.

Surface labels below are this repo's operator surfaces: Collector is the service itself, Dashboards is what you see in Grafana, Deployment is the image and the stacks you run, and Docs is the written guides.

## IMPROVED

- **Auto-discovery collects your cloud-login devices again** `Collector` — If you turn on auto-discovery, the collector now logs in to newer SMART/KLAP devices it finds (like most IOT plugs and strips already did) and collects them, without you having to list each one. 2026.10.3 held those devices back by default; this release collects them by default. If you prefer the stricter behaviour — your TP-Link login going only to devices you list — set `KASA_COLLECTOR_TRUST_DISCOVERED_DEVICES=False`. Either way, your account details are still never written to the logs or sent out in the discovery broadcast.

## FIXED

- **The "trust discovered devices" setting now works for newer devices** `Collector` — In 2026.10.3, turning this setting on (or listing a newer SMART/KLAP device) did not actually bring that device back — it was still skipped. It now logs in and collects as intended. (This corrects the 2026.10.3 note that said the setting restored discovery "as before"; for newer devices it did not.)
- **A newer device that couldn't be reached no longer fills the log with errors** `Collector` — A SMART/KLAP device the collector couldn't connect to used to leave a connection open and log repeated "Unclosed client session" errors every discovery cycle. The connection is now closed cleanly, and an unreachable-but-unlisted device is reported once, with a clear note on how to add it.
- **Documentation** `Docs` — The configuration guide and `.env` example describe the new default and when to turn the hardening back on.
