# 2026.10.3 — 2026-10-25

A security and accuracy release: the collector stops exposing your TP-Link account, and power strips and older plugs now record and chart correctly.

Surface labels below are this repo's operator surfaces: Collector is the service itself, Dashboards is what you see in Grafana, Deployment is the image and the stacks you run, and Docs is the written guides.

## NEW

- **Only devices you trust get your TP-Link login** `Collector` — The collector no longer offers your TP-Link account to every device that answers on the network. It uses your credentials only for devices you list in your configuration (or ones it has logged in to before). If a new cloud-login device appears, it tells you once so you can add it. Set the new trust option if you'd rather it log in to anything it finds, as before.
- **Per-outlet energy for older plugs and strips** `Dashboards` — Older plugs that report in different units now show up on the dashboards instead of reading as zero, and each outlet on a power strip is tracked on its own — even two outlets you happened to give the same name.

## IMPROVED

- **Your account details stay out of the logs** `Collector` `Deployment` — The startup log and the device-login logs no longer print any part of your TP-Link password, your InfluxDB token, or a password inside your InfluxDB address. If you kept container logs from before this release, treat the password and token in them as exposed and change them.
- **Strip and device charts add up correctly** `Dashboards` — A power strip is counted once as a whole, and its outlets are charted separately, so the per-device and per-outlet panels no longer blend or average the two together.
- **Clearer collector health counts** `Dashboards` — A device that answers with a reading the collector can't store is now counted as a device problem, not as a failed write to the database, so the health numbers point at the real cause.
- **Documentation** `Docs` — The configuration, testing, and dashboard guides describe the new trust setting, the stored data, and the current test devices.

## FIXED

- **Old plugs no longer record zero** `Dashboards` — Plugs on older firmware that report current and energy as fractional values were being stored rounded down to whole numbers (a fraction of an amp became 0); their readings are now kept accurately.
- **Two outlets named the same no longer merge** `Dashboards` — If you left two outlets on a strip with the same name, their energy was being combined into one line; each is now shown on its own.
