# 2026.11.0 — 2026-11-05

Your TP-Link login is better protected on your network, SMART power strips show every outlet, and the collector keeps collecting through more of what goes wrong.

The surfaces here are `Collector` (the service you run), `Dashboards` (Grafana) and `Docs`.

## NEW

- **Every outlet of a SMART power strip** `Collector` `Dashboards` — Strips like the P316M and P304M now show each outlet as its own series, the same way HS300 strips always have.
- **Strips recover on their own** `Collector` — If a strip stops reporting one outlet's energy after repeated errors, the collector reconnects it in the background and keeps collecting the other outlets meanwhile.

## IMPROVED

- **Your TP-Link login goes only where it is safe** `Collector` — With auto-discovery on, a device you have not listed is logged in to only over the protocols that do not hand it something it could reuse against your account. Newer KLAP devices are collected as before; a device that asks for an older-style login is named once in the log and collected as soon as you list it in your device hosts.
- **Devices you list by name follow their address** `Collector` — If your router moves a device to a new address, the collector finds it there on the next pass, and stops talking to whatever takes the old address.
- **One slow or missing device no longer slows the rest** `Collector` — Each device gets its own time limit, so your other devices keep their normal sampling interval.
- **A more honest health check** `Collector` — The container only reports healthy when it is actually storing readings, and a long polling interval or a clock change no longer makes a working collector look unhealthy.
- **Less of your home in your data** `Collector` — Your devices' location and Wi-Fi name are no longer written to your database, files or logs.
- **Reliability** `Collector` — Lots of behind-the-scenes hardening of how passwords are kept out of logs, how connections are closed, and how unusual device replies are handled.

## FIXED

- **Power strips on newer firmware showed up as one plug** `Collector` `Dashboards` — An HS300 on the newer secure protocol is collected outlet by outlet again.
- **One device's odd reading could hide other devices' status** `Dashboards` — A device reporting a value in an unexpected form no longer stops other devices' status from being stored.
- **A health setting written like "2m" broke the health check** `Collector` — It is now read the same way the collector reads it.

See `Docs` (configuration) for the device-hosts setting and the health-check timing.
